# Demo personas

The final demo walks these two people through the **same** journey. The contrast is the
point: same flow, very different experience.

## Priya — first-time buyer, anxious

- 29, salaried, first property, has never applied for a mortgage.
- Doesn't know what LTV, amortization, or a stress test are.
- Small deposit (~10%), so the journey should surface the high-LTV warning.
- Uses "What's this?" on almost every field; asks the assistant follow-up questions.
- Success = she finishes understanding what she filled in and why the numbers matter.

## Marek — refinancing, confident

- 44, self-employed, refinancing an existing mortgage to a lower rate.
- Knows the terminology; wants to move fast and skip the explanations.
- Comfortable LTV (~55%), strong income — validation should mostly come back clean.
- Triggers the self-employed branch (extra document requirements).
- Success = he gets to a clean standardized profile in under two minutes.

## What to show

1. Priya hits the property step, opens three explanations, gets the high-LTV warning,
   adjusts the down payment, sees the warning clear.
2. Marek blasts through, self-employed branch adds two documents, profile is clean.
3. Side by side: the same `review` screen, different read-back, different risk list.
